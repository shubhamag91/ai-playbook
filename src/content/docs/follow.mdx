The AI landscape moves fast. These are the people, newsletters, and shows worth adding to your reading list.

## Researchers & Educators

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Andrej Karpathy" icon="user">
    Ex-OpenAI Research Director · Ex-Tesla AI
    
    The best educator on how neural networks actually work. "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" is the gold standard.
    
    [X @karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy) · [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@karpathy) · [Blog](https://karpathy.ai)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lilian Weng" icon="user">
    VP of Research Safety · OpenAI
    
    Writes the most thorough deep-dives on AI topics — agents, RLHF, diffusion models, alignment.
    
    [X @lilianweng](https://x.com/lilianweng) · [Blog](https://lilianweng.github.io/blog/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sebastian Raschka" icon="user">

    [X @rasht](https://x.com/rasbt) · [Ahead of AI](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="François Chollet" icon="user">

  <Card title="Jeremy Howard" icon="user">

## Newsletters

### Daily Digests

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The Rundown AI" icon="mail">
    Daily · Free · rundown.ai
    
    Concise daily briefing on AI news, tools, research. Good starting point for beginners.
    
    [Subscribe](https://rundown.ai/) · [X @therundownai](https://x.com/therundownai)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ben's Bites" icon="mail">
    Daily · Free + Pro · bensbites.co
    
    Tracks new AI product launches and research papers. Strong taste for signal vs noise.
    
    [Subscribe](https://www.bensbites.co/) · [X @bensbites](https://x.com/bensbites)
  </Card>
  <Card title="TLDR AI" icon="mail">
    Daily · Free · tldr.tech/ai
    
    Three-paragraph summaries of top AI stories. Highly scannable.
    
    [Subscribe](https://tldr.tech/ai)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

### Weekly Deep-Dives

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The Batch" icon="mail">
    Weekly · Free · deeplearning.ai/the-batch
    
    Andrew Ng's weekly newsletter. Covers research, industry news with genuine expertise.
    
    [Subscribe](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="One Useful Thing" icon="mail">
    Weekly · Free · oneusefulthing.org
    
    Ethan Mollick's Substack. Practical, evidence-based. Best for using AI better.
    
    [Subscribe](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Latent Space" icon="mail">
    Weekly · Free · latent.space
    
    For AI engineers. Deep dives into model releases, fine-tuning, infrastructure.
    
    [Subscribe](https://latent.space)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ahead of AI" icon="mail">
    Monthly · Free · sebastianraschka.com
    
    Sebastian Raschka's review of important AI/ML papers. Exceptionally educational.
    
    [Subscribe](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
