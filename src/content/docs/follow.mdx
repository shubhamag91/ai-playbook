## How to Stay Current (May 2026)

**Quick updates:** Ben's Bites or The Rundown AI (5 min/day)

**Deep learning:** Andrej Karpathy on YouTube (weekly)

**Practical use cases:** One Useful Thing (weekly)

**Engineering focus:** Latent Space newsletter + podcast (weekly)

**Research papers:** Ahead of AI (monthly)

**Safety & alignment:** Interconnects + Dwarkesh Patel podcast (bi-weekly)

**All of the above:** Pick 2-3 newsletters and 1-2 YouTube channels. Set a weekly review time.
