## January 2026

### DeepSeek V4 Release (Jan 8)
- **Reasoning capability** matching GPT-4o on most benchmarks
- **Open weights** under MIT license
- **Cost:** $0.55/$2.19 per 1M tokens (API)
- **Impact:** Open-weight reasoning at frontier quality

### Llama 4 Release (Jan 15)
- MoE architecture, 405B total params
- Better instruction-following
- Faster inference than Llama 3
- **Cost:** Free (self-host), MIT license

## February 2026

### Claude 4 Opus (Feb 3)
- **400K context window**
- **Agent mode by default**
- **99.2% HumanEval accuracy**
- **Cost:** $15/$75 per 1M tokens

### Mistral Large 2 (Feb 10)
- **Better multilingual** support (20+ languages)
- **Context:** 128K tokens
- **Cost:** $2/$6 per 1M tokens

### Gemini 2.0 Pro (Feb 20)
- **Multimodal reasoning**
- **Video understanding** (12 fps streaming)
- **1M context window**
- **Cost:** $10/$30 per 1M tokens (free tier: 150M/day)

## March 2026

### GPT-4 Turbo Retirement (Mar 1)
- OpenAI discontinued GPT-4 Turbo
- Users migrated to GPT-4o or GPT-5 series

### Qwen 3.0 Release (Mar 5)
- Alibaba's reasoning model
- **Open weights**
- **170B parameters**
- Competitive with DeepSeek V4

### Open-Source Surge
- Phi-5 (Microsoft) — small but strong
- Falcon 3.0 (TII UAE)

## April 2026

### Anthropic Releases Constitutional AI Toolkit (Apr 10)
- **Open-source** methodology for training aligned models
- Anyone can now fine-tune Claude-like behavior into open models
- Impact: Democratizes alignment research

### GPT-5.5 Preview (Apr 15)
- OpenAI teased GPT-5.5 capabilities
- **Promised:** Better reasoning, true multimodal, faster
- **Release:** May 2026 (see below)

### Kling AI v2 (Apr 20)
- Video generation from text
- Rivals Sora on quality
- More accessible (cheaper, open signup)

### DeepSeek V4 Flash & V4 Pro (Apr 22)
- **V4 Flash:** Ultra-cheap at $0.14/$0.28 per 1M tokens
- **V4 Pro:** Premium variant for design/coding at $0.55/$2.19
- Both MIT-licensed, open weights

### Muse Spark (Meta) (Apr 28)
- Meta's latest open-weight model, replacing Llama
- Strong design capabilities (1312 Elo on Design Arena)
- MIT license, runs locally

### Qwen 3.6 (Alibaba) (Apr 30)
- Alibaba's flagship model
- Strong across coding, math, reasoning, and vision
- Competitive pricing at ~$0.40/$1.50 per 1M tokens

## For Builders

**Pick a model based on:**
1. **What you're building** (chat? code? multimodal?)
2. **Your constraints** (speed? cost? privacy?)
3. **Your fallback** (have a 2nd choice if primary is down)

**2026 advantage:** Redundancy is cheap. Use multiple models. If one fails or gets slow, swap to another. For model comparison, see [Model Specs & Pricing](/reference/model-specs/). For cost analysis, see [Economics of AI](/reference/economics-of-ai/).
