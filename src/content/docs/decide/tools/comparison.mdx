## Quick Notes

### Chat Interfaces
Best for research, brainstorming, writing, and reasoning. All offer free trials.

### Coding Tools
Most include both local IDE extensions and cloud-based alternatives. Pick based on your workflow (terminal-first vs IDE-first).

### Content Creation
Image, video, music, and voice tools. Pricing varies widely  -  most offer free trials or freemium tiers.

### APIs & Frameworks
For building AI applications programmatically. Pay-per-token models scale from hobby projects to millions of requests.

### Models
Major LLMs compared by pricing and speed. Sort by cost to find the cheapest, or by speed to find the fastest. For detailed specs, see [Models Guide](/decide/models/guide).
