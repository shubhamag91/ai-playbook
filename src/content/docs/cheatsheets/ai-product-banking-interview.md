## Banking Domain for PMs

### Banking Products & Services

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **What are the main banking product categories?** | Deposits (savings, fixed deposits), Loans (personal, home, auto, credit card), Cards (debit, credit), Wealth Management, Insurance, Payments (UPI, transfers). |
| **What is the difference between a bank and a fintech?** | Bank: regulated, deposit insurance, physical presence, traditional. Fintech: tech-first, often focused on specific use cases, may partner with banks. |
| **What is a neobank?** | Digital-only bank with no physical branches. Examples: RazorpayX, Jupiter, Niyo. |
| **What is UPI and how does it work?** | Unified Payments Interface - real-time payment system in India. Link bank accounts, scan QR, send money via app. |

## AI Product Questions in Banking

### Product Design

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **How would you design an AI-powered personal loan product?** | Define target segment, credit assessment approach (traditional + alternative data), approval workflow, pricing model, risk thresholds. Key: balance approval speed with risk. |
| **Design an AI fraud detection feature for a banking app.** | Real-time alerts, transaction categorization, customizable controls, educational content. Key: balance security with user experience. |
| **How would you build an AI investment advisor (robo-advisor)?** | Risk profiling, goal-based investing, automated rebalancing, tax-loss harvesting. Key: regulatory compliance, suitability. |
| **Design an AI-powered customer service chatbot for a bank.** | Handle common queries (balance, transactions), escalate complex issues, integrate with CRM. Key: handle sensitive data securely, maintain trust. |

### Banking-Specific Metrics

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **How would you measure success of an AI loan product?** | Approval rate, disbursement time, default rate, customer satisfaction, conversion rate, NIM. |
| **What metrics for AI fraud detection?** | Fraud caught rate, false positive rate, customer complaint rate, alert response time. |
| **How do you measure AI recommendation success?** | Click-through rate, conversion, basket size, revenue per user, engagement. |
| **What are guardrail metrics for banking AI?** | Compliance rate, data privacy incidents, customer complaint rate, regulatory findings. |

## Regulatory Considerations

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **What regulatory considerations for AI in banking?** | Data privacy (RBI, GDPR), model explainability (fair lending laws), audit trails, board approvals, model risk management. |
| **How do you ensure fair lending with AI?** | Test for bias across demographics, use interpretable models, maintain documentation, regular audits. |
| **What is the RBI guidelines for AI in banking?** | Customer consent for data usage, algorithmic fairness, grievance redressal, board oversight of AI systems. |
| **How do you handle model risk in banking?** | Documentation, validation, monitoring, governance framework, regulatory reporting. |

## Case Study Questions

### Case 1: AI-Powered Credit Card
- Design an AI feature for a new credit card
- How would you determine credit limits dynamically?
- How do you personalize offers based on spending patterns?

### Case 2: Payment Fraud
- Competitor launched real-time fraud alerts
- How do you respond? What's your differentiated approach?

### Case 3: Customer Experience
- Customers complain about slow loan approval
- How would you use AI to improve while managing risk?

### Case 4: Regulatory Change
- New RBI guideline on AI model explainability
- How do you ensure compliance across existing AI products?

## Quick Reference Cards

### Banking AI Product Checklist

- [ ] Clear use case and business value
- [ ] Data availability and quality
- [ ] Regulatory compliance assessment
- [ ] Model risk management plan
- [ ] Customer trust and transparency
- [ ] Success metrics defined
- [ ] Rollback strategy

### Key Stakeholders in Banking AI

| Stakeholder | Concern |
|-------------|---------|
| **Risk Team** | Model validation, regulatory compliance |
| **Compliance** | Data privacy, fair lending |
| **IT** | Integration, security |
| **Business** | Revenue, customer satisfaction |
| **Executive** | ROI, strategic fit |

### Common Banking AI Failures

- Biased credit models → regulatory issues
- Poor fraud false positives → customer complaints
- Unclear AI decisions → customer trust loss
- Over-reliance on AI → operational risk
