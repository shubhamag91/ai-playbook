## Writing & Content

### For Drafting Fast
- **Claude.ai** — Best for long-form writing, essays, reports. 200K context = entire documents at once.
- **ChatGPT** — Faster iteration, good for short-form. Web search built in.
- **Grammarly** — Real-time grammar + style. Works in your browser, email, documents.

**Workflow:** Draft in Claude, edit in Grammarly, publish. Takes half the time.

---

### For Research & Synthesis
- **NotebookLM** — Upload 300 PDFs, ask questions, get summaries and audio notes. Free. Game-changer.
- **Perplexity** — Web search + cited sources. When you need "prove it."
- **Gemini** — 1M context = entire research database in one conversation.

**Workflow:** Gather sources → NotebookLM for synthesis → Claude for writing → Done in 2 hours instead of 2 days

---

### For Editing & Polish
- **Claude.ai** — Best at deep editing (voice, flow, structure). Ask it to rewrite.
- **Grammarly** — Catches grammar, tone, clarity as you type.
- **ChatGPT** — Quick line edits, alternative phrasings.

**Pro tip:** Use Claude for substantive editing, Grammarly for polish.

## Design & Image Creation

### For Generating Images
- **Midjourney** — Highest quality. $10-60/mo. Use when results matter.
- **DALL-E 3** — In ChatGPT. Included with Plus. Quick iterations.
- **Flux** — Open-source, free locally. No API costs at scale.

**Cost calculation:** Midjourney if you do 20+ images/month. DALL-E for casual use.

---

### For Real Design Work
- **Figma + Claude** — Describe design in Claude, paste into Figma. Iterate fast.
- **Cursor** — Design tokens, components, CSS generation.

**Reality:** AI isn't replacing designers yet, but it's accelerating the boring parts.

## Video & Motion

### Quick Videos
- **Runway** — Edit + AI generation. $12+/mo. Full control.
- **Sora** (via ChatGPT Pro) — Text → video. Cinematic quality. ~30 sec clips.
- **Veo 3.1** — Fast, included with Gemini Ultra.

**Timeline:** Sora is ~30 seconds. Runway is real editing. Choose based on scope.

---

### Video Editing
- **Runway** — The Swiss Army knife. Generate, edit, upscale, motion blur.
- **CapCut** — Free, AI upscaler, captions, cuts.

## Music & Audio

### Music Generation
- **Suno** — "Create a lo-fi hip hop beat for studying." 3 minutes later, you have it. Free + $10/mo.
- **Udio** — Alternative to Suno. Similar quality.

**Use case:** Background music, royalty-free tracks, prototypes. Not for professional albums.

---

### Voiceovers & TTS
- **ElevenLabs** — Natural-sounding voices, 29 languages. $5+/mo.
- **OpenAI TTS** — Simple, cheap, less natural.
- **Suno** — Singing + speaking. Fun for creative projects.

**Quality:** ElevenLabs > OpenAI TTS

## Workflow Templates

### Write an Article (4 hours instead of 8)
1. Gather sources (30 min)
2. NotebookLM: Upload PDFs, generate outline (15 min)
3. Claude.ai: Draft sections using outline (2 hours)
4. Grammarly: Polish (30 min)
5. ChatGPT: Alternative headlines (15 min)
→ **Done in 4 hours** (was 8 with traditional research)

### Create a Presentation (2 hours instead of 6)
1. Claude.ai: Brainstorm outline (15 min)
2. Cursor: Generate slides via code (45 min)
3. DALL-E 3: Create 3 visuals (30 min)
4. Polish in Figma or PowerPoint (30 min)
→ **Done in 2 hours**

### Automate Your Inbox
1. Zapier: Flag → Gmail label + Notion database
2. Zapier: Important email → Slack notification + summary
3. Zapier: Form submission → Google Sheet + Thank you email
→ **Saves 3 hours/week** (no manual organization)

## Pro Tips for Productivity

1. **Batch similar tasks** — Write all summaries at once, not one-by-one
2. **Use Claude for thinking, ChatGPT for doing** — Claude for strategy, ChatGPT for quick execution
3. **Automate the repetitive** — If you do it twice, automate it
4. **Don't optimize tools, optimize workflows** — The best tool is useless if it doesn't fit your process
5. **Free tiers first** — Try NotebookLM, Suno, Figma free before paying
